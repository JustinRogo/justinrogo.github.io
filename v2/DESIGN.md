# Design System: Justin Rogowski V2

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One workspace, three printings"**

V2 is one site with one content model, one route map and one component tree, printed in three site styles chosen under Settings → Site style and applied as `:root[data-style]`. **Botanical** is the default (`data-style="botanical"`, also the value an unknown or missing preference resolves to); **Original** is the blue/orange glass design, kept intact for anyone who chooses it (`data-style="original"`); **Risograph** prints the site in fluorescent pink, cobalt and yellow ink on paper (`data-style="riso"`). Each style has its own light and dark printing: Original's Light/Dark, Botanical's Sunlit/Moonlit, Risograph's Cream paper/Black paper. The root `index.html` pre-paint script sets `data-style`, the browser chrome colour and the style's display-face stylesheet before first paint, so no style flashes another.

The unscoped base (`glass-theme.css`, `styles.css`, `layout.css`) is Original. Botanical (`botanical/botanical.css`) and Risograph (`riso/riso.css`) are complete overlay layers: every rule they ship is scoped under their own `data-style` selector (the shared Site style picker and the page-background art are the only unscoped exceptions), and they reach the shared components by remapping the shared custom properties (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--blue`, `--orange`, `--glass-*`, `--panel-shadow`, `--accent-fill*`) rather than by forking markup. Frontmatter keys without a prefix are Original; `botanical-` and `riso-` keys belong to those styles, and `-dark-` keys are each style's dark printing.

**Original — "Personal glass workspace."** Ice-blue or deep navy ground, refractive glass, blue light, orange actions and orbital geometry give practical information work a vivid interactive setting. Geist and consistent line icons keep the material system legible. Tech Text, Magic Bento, Branched Menu and Glass Surface define the experience; the atmosphere drifts when effects are enabled.

**Botanical — "The conservatory."** A lush, earthy field journal: matte grained paper panels on a parchment ground, a deep forest-green rail like a journal's binding, Fraunces display type with a soft optical axis, leaf-cut corners, and living plants drawn in SVG (ferns, monstera, pothos, tradescantia) that grow, unfurl and sway. Pip, the resident pothos, lengthens as you scroll and can be watered. Moonlit turns the paper to deep green and swaps the green accent for amber.

**Risograph — "The print run."** Every view is a sheet pulled from a three-drum press: warm newsprint with fibre grain, fluorescent pink, cobalt and golden-yellow inks that overprint where they meet, and hierarchy carried by ink, scale and overprint instead of panels and shadows. Archivo condensed caps set names, headings and big figures; display type and prints sit a plate off register and pull into register on hover. Black paper keeps the same inks, mixed opaque for dark stock.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Three complete site styles over one component tree, switched by `:root[data-style]`, each with a light and a dark printing.
- Original: ice-blue/navy glass with orange actions, blue illumination and refraction behind content.
- Botanical (default): grained paper, forest-green binding, Fraunces display, leaf-cut corners and living SVG plants.
- Risograph: newsprint, three overprinting inks, condensed Archivo caps, 2px corners, 1.5px ink rules and deliberate misregistration.
- The CliftonStrengths domain colours are product data and stay identical in every style.
- Every style honours Calm, reduced motion, high contrast and forced colours by removing ornament, never content.

## Colors

Each style owns its palette; the shared components read it through remapped custom properties, so a token's role (page, surface, ink, accent) is constant while its ink changes.

### Primary

- **Original — action orange** (`action-orange`, `action-orange-hover`, `action-ink`): filled primary buttons retain this fixed palette across themes. Active filters use the observed `filter-orange` and `filter-ink` pair. **Semantic orange** (`orange`, `dark-orange`) marks current navigation, branch traces, the wordmark arrow, saved hearts and quiet emphasis. **Bento glow** (`bento-glow`) is the selected-work spotlight.
- **Botanical — conservatory green** (`botanical-green`, `botanical-green-hover`): Sunlit's primary fill (buttons, pressed filters, current tool tab), ordinary links and focus, with paper (`botanical-paper`) reversed out of it. **Moonlit amber** (`botanical-dark-amber`, `-hover`, `-amber-ink`) replaces it on dark paper, where green would sink into the page; the portrait's contact circles take the same amber in Moonlit.
- **Risograph — fluorescent pink** (`riso-pink`, `riso-pink-hover`): the primary pull, the hero's cut block, rules under section titles and the pink plate. Pink fills carry dark navy text (`riso-ink-on-color`). Text set in pink uses the deeper mixes: `riso-pink-display` for the large name and `riso-pink-text` for small text, active-filter borders and the focus ring.

### Secondary

- **Original — blue illumination** (`blue`, `blue-hover`, `tint`, dark counterparts) and **tech blue** (`tech-accent`): role text, ordinary focus rings, supporting surfaces and Tech Text's pointer accent.
- **Botanical — terracotta and ochre** (`botanical-terracotta-text`, `botanical-terracotta`, `botanical-ochre`): terracotta is the semantic "orange" (italic name line, greeting emphasis, text-link hover, strength ranks); raw terracotta dots titles and fills pots and berries; ochre marks the wordmark sprig on the rail. **Rail accent** (`botanical-rail-accent`, `botanical-rail-active`) is the pale gold of current navigation on the green binding.
- **Risograph — cobalt** (`riso-cobalt`, `riso-cobalt-deep`): the rail and phone dock are a cobalt flood (`riso-dark-rail` on Black paper); cobalt sets ROGOWSKI, links, secondary-button rules and frames; deep cobalt is Cream paper's body ink. **Golden yellow** (`riso-yellow`): current navigation, pressed filters, the "How can I help?" block, selection and the portrait disc, always with `riso-ink-on-color` text (`riso-on-yellow-muted` for its secondary text). `riso-duo-blue` is the third duotone ink for project screenshots.

### Neutral

- **Original:** ice/navy page (`bg`, `dark-bg`), solid surface (`surface`, `dark-surface`), ink and supporting ink (`ink`, `muted`), rules and branches (`line`, `branch-line`), glass fill/edge (`glass-fill`, `glass-edge`), `nav` for hover and segmented surfaces; `contrast-*` are high-contrast overrides.

- **All styles — CliftonStrengths domains** (`strength-thinking`, `strength-relationship`, `strength-executing`, `strength-influencing`, `strength-light-ink`): product data, never remapped by a style.

**The Semantic Glass Rule.** In Original, use live theme variables for glass, text, and supporting UI; retain the implemented fixed orange primary-action palette.

**The Remap, Don't Fork Rule.** A style changes the shared components by redefining the shared custom properties under its own `:root[data-style]` selector, scoped to that selector, so the other two styles stay pixel-identical.

**The Deep Ink Rule.** In Risograph, fluorescent pink and yellow are fills and shapes; text that must be read is set in `riso-pink-text`, `riso-pink-display`, cobalt or navy, and anything printed on pink or yellow carries `riso-ink-on-color`.

**The Fixed Domains Rule.** The four CliftonStrengths domain colours are data and print the same in every style and theme.

## Elevation & Depth

**Original** — glass is structural. Floating rails, headers, hero and lower panels use theme-specific panel shadows; project lists and task surfaces use translucent fills, blur and larger curves; bento adds reflected rims and pointer illumination. Dialogs are solid with the modal shadow. GlassLayer uses the shared Glass Surface SVG renderer behind controls (border factor 0.05, map brightness 42%, map opacity 0.51, frost 0.2, displacement −110, red offset 5). Fine pointers may refract; touch and reduced motion use frosted glass; reduced transparency, high contrast and forced colours make layers opaque. Backgrounds: Shape Waves, Antigravity, Fractal, Aurora, Orbits and None (device default Shape Waves above 800px, Aurora below).

**Botanical** — lifted paper. Refraction and backdrop blur are off (GlassLayer renders paper in any non-Original style). Panels are grained paper with a 1px sand rim and a soft two-part shadow; the hero adds a printed plate rule inset 7px; dialogs layer a deep shadow, a 1px edge and a 7px paper margin with a plate rule, over a green-tinted backdrop blurred 3px. Primary buttons are pressed-in: a top highlight, a 2px bottom inner shade and a soft shadow tinted from the fill; `:active` sinks 1px. The rail casts its own green-black shadow. Backgrounds: Conservatory (layered foliage in a slow breeze, with sun or fireflies; the default), Herbarium (pressed specimens), Canopy light, and None.

**Risograph** — flat ink, no shadows. `--shadow` and `--panel-shadow` are `none`; panels are areas of the same sheet divided by a 1.5px ink rule (`inset 0 0 0 1.5px`, cobalt; pink around the hero and AI Corner). Depth comes only from overprint: shapes and inks multiply on Cream paper and screen on Black paper, solid floods carry a paper-coloured mottle where the ink did not quite cover, and the whole page carries static grain and fibre. Backgrounds: Cut shapes (the default), Halftone, Press sheet (crop marks, registration targets, ink bars) and None.

Each style keeps its own background choice, so switching styles never overwrites another style's selection; only the selected renderer mounts.

### Named Rules

**The Opaque Escape Rule.** Preserve the implemented fallbacks so readability and operation remain available when refraction, transparency, or motion is suppressed.

**The Flat Sheet Rule.** In Risograph, nothing casts a shadow: separation is a 1.5px ink rule, a solid ink flood, or an overprint.

**The Overprint Rule.** Risograph inks that overlap blend (`multiply` on Cream paper, `screen` on Black paper, through `--r-blend`); never stack them opaquely when they are meant to overprint.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** scope every style-specific rule under its own `:root[data-style="…"]` selector and reach shared components by remapping the shared custom properties.
- **Do** give each style both printings: Light/Dark for Original, Sunlit/Moonlit for Botanical, Cream paper/Black paper for Risograph.
- **Do** keep the CliftonStrengths domain colours identical in every style and theme.
- **Do** use ice-blue/navy glass with readable orange actions and blue illumination in Original, and keep Glass Surface refraction behind text and controls.
- **Do** use the actual Tech Text, Magic Bento and Branched Menu implementations for signature interactions in Original.
- **Do** set Botanical headings, legends and the wordmark in Fraunces over Geist body, on grained paper with leaf-cut or pill corners and the forest-green rail.
- **Do** keep Botanical's plants decorative SVG (`aria-hidden`) and let them grow, unfurl and sway only when motion is welcome.
- **Do** set Risograph names, headings and big figures in condensed (62%) Archivo caps, and let dates and numbers be the large type.
- **Do** separate Risograph surfaces with 1.5px ink rules and solid floods, overprint inks through `--r-blend`, and put text on pink or yellow in `riso-ink-on-color`.
- **Do** confine Risograph misregistration to display type and prints (the name, view titles, the portrait plates) and pull it into register on hover or focus.
- **Do** drop ornament (plants, prints, plates, grain, texture) under high contrast and forced colours, and keep content, state and navigation available when effects are off.
- **Do** retain native project links, visible navigation labels and title-first index rows in every style.

### Don't:

- **Don't** let a Botanical or Risograph rule leak outside its `data-style` scope; Original and the other styles must stay pixel-identical.
- **Don't** propagate the V2 system into V1 or root design context.
- **Don't** distort text or controls inside Original's refraction layer.
- **Don't** reintroduce glass refraction or backdrop blur on Botanical or Risograph surfaces.
- **Don't** give Risograph surfaces a box-shadow; depth there is ink, rule and overprint.
- **Don't** set small Risograph text in fluorescent `riso-pink` or `riso-yellow`.
- **Don't** replace real portfolio content with vendor demo tiles or decorative metrics.
- **Don't** rely on optional effects to expose content, state, or navigation.
