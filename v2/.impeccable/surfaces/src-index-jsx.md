# V2 site style: Risograph

Scope: a third V2 site style beside Botanical (default) and Original, chosen in Settings → Site style. Covers the whole V2 app: rail, top bar, Home, Projects, Tools, About, More, settings sheet, its own page backgrounds, Light/Dark. Mode: Experience (personal site; the work and the person lead). Botanical and Original stay pixel-identical.

Audience and job: colleagues, collaborators and the curious meeting Justin and opening his tools and experiments. Same content, routes, integrations and accessibility as the other styles.

User answers (2026-11-18): dark = the same inks on black paper stock; layout may recompose emphasis (giant two-ink name, colour blocks, oversized numerals, asymmetric spans) without changing content or order; whole V2 site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Justin's site as a risograph print run: every view is a sheet pulled from a two/three-drum press, so hierarchy comes from ink, scale and overprint instead of panels and shadows. Refuses the neutral card grid wearing a pink accent.

OWN-WORLD: Warm newsprint (#f2e9d4) with static fibre grain; fluorescent pink, cobalt blue and golden-yellow inks that multiply where they overlap (purple, orange, green overprints); black stock in dark mode. Archivo at 62% width, 800–900 weight, uppercase for names, headings and big numbers; Archivo body. 1.5px ink rules, 2px corners, flat circles/half-discs/triangles, halftone dot fields, a slight plate offset on display type only.

STORY: Meet Justin as a poster, see what he can do for you on a yellow block, read the day and the posts by their big numerals, open featured work as one-ink prints that come up in full colour, then browse the rest of the site in the same press.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name fills the left two-thirds in condensed caps, JUSTIN in pink stacked tight over ROGOWSKI in cobalt, whose pink plate prints slightly out of register; the cut pink block overprints the end of the name. Right: the portrait printed as a blue/pink halftone duotone inside a yellow disc, with ink-stamp contact buttons. Role, intro and the pink "Explore my projects" primary action sit under the name. Below: "How can I help?" as a full yellow block with big condensed item labels (no index numerals: they carry no information) beside My Day and AI Corner, whose dates are the big figures.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — world: "add a 3rd site style … to design it using the Risograph Web System design direction … Treat the reference as a design system, not a screenshot to reproduce."; first-surface experience, from the confirmed answers: "Whole V2 site" and "Same pages, content, and order, but Riso may change emphasis". Signature interaction: registration. Display type and prints sit a plate off register and pull into register on hover/focus. Switching to Riso feeds the new sheet through the press: a top-to-bottom wipe. Motion grammar: short, mechanical, drum-like steps; nothing floats.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
