Telecommuting appeals

Under Article 16.5, UCPEA employees can request review of a full denial, approval for fewer than two remote days per workweek, or permanent revocation. The most recent performance evaluation must be rated Good or better.

Check your situation

What happens after a decision

  1. File with Human Resources. Submit a written appeal within four weeks of receiving the decision. UCPEA says the clock for full or partial denials already issued at launch began September 15, 2026, when the Kuali form became available.
  2. Panel review. HR convenes a two-person panel within two weeks of receiving a timely appeal, with one HR member and one UCPEA appointee. Within two weeks of completing its review, the panel upholds the decision or recommends reconsideration.
  3. Final determination, if referred. If the panel is divided or recommends reconsideration, the vice president or designee issues a final written decision within two weeks.

Position audits

A position audit checks whether your classification still fits your duties. Career progression under Article 31.2 recognizes proficiency and development within your current position.

Before you request an audit

  • A majority of your duties have changed enough that your classification no longer describes the work.
  • You’ve performed the changed duties consistently for at least two months.
  • You’ve completed your first year in this position and, if you were reclassified, at least a year has passed.

Meet with your supervisor. Prepare a description of your current duties, a current résumé, and an organizational chart for the Kuali request.

Use the date on HR’s confirmation of receipt.

From submission to written result

  1. Submit and get acknowledgment. The supervisory chain has four weeks from receipt of your form to acknowledge it and return it to HR.
  2. Wait for HR’s assessment. HR has 12 weeks from receipt of the completed job inventory to provide written results. UCPEA and UConn may agree to extend Article 31.3 time limits.
  3. Check the effective date. Article 31.3(e) uses the date you submitted the position audit form. HR’s webpage says 60 days before HR’s official receipt of the completed packet. Ask UCPEA to confirm the date that applies.

If the request stalls or is denied

Supervisor or manager declines to support the request
UConn HR says you can submit your packet to Workforce Solutions for independent review.
HR denies the request after an audit
Request a meeting with the classification specialist and the Chief Human Resources Officer or designee. You have 14 calendar days after the meeting to submit additional evidence. Article 31.3 provides for a final written response within six weeks.

    Professional Development

    UCPEA’s Professional Development Award helps cover eligible activities you choose. UCPEA lists a $1,000 maximum per applicant for FY 2026–27.

    What’s covered
    Conferences, workshops, training, and professional dues may qualify. Certificates require committee review. License renewals, admission fees, and standardized tests are excluded.
    Training required for your job
    Article 34.3 requires the University to pay for work-related training that management requires. Ask your department about payment before using the award.
    When to apply
    Submit the request at least 15 days before the activity or membership due date. Submit reimbursement or expense reallocation within 28 calendar days after the activity ends.
    What to prepare
    Have the activity dates and costs ready. Coordinate payment and any expense reallocation with your department’s fiscal officer.

    Childcare Reimbursement

    Employees in UCPEA-represented positions can request partial reimbursement for eligible childcare expenses. The child must be a dependent under 13, with an exception for a documented disability.

    What’s covered
    UCPEA considers eligible expenses from state-licensed daycare providers and eligible summer day camps. The program considers up to $5,000 in expenses per filing period. If both parents apply, each must claim different expenses.
    What to submit
    Use UCPEA’s application and receipt form and gather itemized receipts for the expenses you’re claiming.
    When to file
    January–April expenses are due May 15, May–August expenses are due September 15, and September–December expenses are due January 15 of the following year. UCPEA moves weekend and holiday deadlines to the next business day.
    Contract funding
    The annual reimbursement pool is $175,000 starting July 2026, $225,000 starting July 2027, and $250,000 starting July 2028.

    Tuition Benefits

    A waiver reduces eligible tuition charges. Reimbursement pays back eligible expenses after successful completion. UConn HR generally requires at least 50% employment for the options below.

    Employee tuition waiver
    Use for eligible UConn fall or spring courses, space permitting. Graduate coursework must be part of a degree program or work-related. Apply each semester by the tenth day of classes, subject to HR’s late-registration rules. Employee waiver form
    Dependent child tuition waiver
    Use for a child enrolled in a UConn undergraduate degree program who qualifies as your tax dependent and is under 24 throughout the calendar year. Apply each semester for each child. HR lists June 1 for fall and November 15 for spring. Have proof of age and dependency ready for the first request. Dependent child waiver form
    Employee tuition reimbursement
    Use for eligible career-related coursework at an accredited institution when a waiver does not apply. Apply at the beginning of the term. Submit your paid fee bill and grade report by September 15 for summer, January 15 for fall, or June 1 for spring. Employee reimbursement form
    Reciprocal tuition
    Eligible dependent children enrolled at the University of Rhode Island or University of Maine at Orono may qualify for in-state tuition through UConn HR.
    Review UConn HR instructions

    Scholarships

    UCPEA offers three $1,300 scholarships to dependent children of UCPEA members. These were the eligibility criteria for the latest round listed by UCPEA.

    Sandy Schulte Memorial Scholarship
    For high school seniors age 17 or older entering a four-year institution as full-time students. UCPEA considers academic achievement and school and community involvement.
    Austin Leadership Scholarship
    For full-time students enrolled in a UConn degree program. UCPEA considers academic achievement and leadership.
    Roslyn Swift Scholarship
    For high school seniors age 17 or older pursuing a credential, certificate, or two-year degree through a vocational or technical school, community college, or certification program.

    The latest published deadline was April 21, 2026, for the 2026–27 academic year. Check UCPEA’s page for the next round’s dates, required materials, and application links.

    Check UCPEA scholarship applications

    Hardship Fund

    UCPEA’s FY 2026–27 program offers grants of up to $500 to eligible union members facing financial hardship. Salary does not determine the grant amount.

    Who can apply
    UCPEA members who did not receive a grant in the preceding fiscal year. Members who received a grant in FY 2025–26 can next qualify in FY 2027–28, subject to the rules then in effect.
    FY 2026–27 application periods
    UCPEA lists July–October, November–February, and March–June. For FY 2026–27, the November–February period includes the funds from the missed July–October period. UCPEA reviews applications as they come in.
    If funds run out
    Check availability on UCPEA’s site. If UCPEA cannot fund your request, you can apply again during the next application period. Applications do not carry over automatically.
    Check fund availability and apply

    Sick Leave Bank

    Article 11.3 allows eligible employees to request donated paid time while on approved federal or state FMLA or SEBAC supplemental leave.

    Qualifying reasons
    The bank may cover the employee’s own serious health condition or care for a spouse or dependent child with a qualifying serious health condition.
    Leave balances
    Other applicable paid leave must generally be used first, including sick, personal, holiday, and compensatory time. Employees may keep up to two weeks of vacation. Caretaker requests also require using the applicable 10 sick days under Article 11.1(a).
    How much time is available
    Article 11.3 caps bank time at 75 days in a rolling two-year period, subject to the applicable leave entitlement.
