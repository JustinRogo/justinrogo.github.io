Workplace support

Start with a steward or the UCPEA office

You do not need to decide whether an issue is a formal grievance before asking for guidance. A union representative can help you understand the process and applicable timelines.

  1. 1

    Write down the factsKeep relevant dates, messages, documents, and the names of people involved.

  2. 2

    Contact UCPEA promptlyContractual timelines can be short, so do not wait for the situation to resolve itself.

  3. 3

    Review the governing languageArticles 23 and 41 cover grievances and representation rights.
